# Requirements

## 1. Business Requirements
- **Business Purpose**: Provide a premium, official digital storefront for Rebel Season by Riya Roopwani, a fashion/clothing brand.
- **Target Customers**: Fashion-forward individuals seeking sophisticated, premium clothing with a modern feminine aesthetic.
- **E-commerce Objectives**: Facilitate direct-to-consumer sales, build brand identity outside of Instagram, streamline order processing, and provide a seamless shopping experience.

## 2. Customer Journey & Workflow
- **Discovery**: Customers discover products via the homepage, category pages, or direct links from social media.
- **Shopping**: Customers view high-quality product images, select variants (size, color), and add items to their cart.
- **Checkout**: A frictionless checkout process collecting contact info, shipping, and payment.
- **Post-Purchase**: Customers receive order confirmation, tracking updates, and can view their order history in their account.

## 3. Customer Features (V1)
- Browse products with filtering and sorting.
- View detailed product pages (images, descriptions, variants).
- Add to cart and manage cart items.
- Checkout with Razorpay integration.
- Shipping address management.
- Customer account creation, login, and order history.
- Search functionality.

## 4. Admin Features (V1)
- **Dashboard**: Overview of revenue, recent orders, and stock alerts.
- **Product Management**: Create, edit, delete products, manage variants, and upload images to Cloudinary.
- **Category Management**: Organize products.
- **Order Management**: View and update order statuses, track Shiprocket shipments.
- **Inventory**: Monitor and adjust variant-level stock.
- **Customers**: View customer details and order history.

## 5. Technical Requirements
- **Performance**: High Core Web Vitals, optimized images, fast server-side rendering.
- **Security**: Protection against SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, secure admin routes, server-side validation.
- **Scalability**: Architecture supporting future abstraction into microservices if needed.
- **SEO**: Dynamic metadata, Open Graph tags, canonical URLs, sitemap, structured data.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG-conscious, semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, screen-reader friendly.
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first design, optimized for all breakpoints.
