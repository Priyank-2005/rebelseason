# UI Design System

## 1. Brand Direction
- **Aesthetic**: Feminine but not childish, Fashion-forward, Premium, Minimal, Editorial, Clean, Modern, Sophisticated.
- **Primary Vibe**: Light pink accents, ample white space, strong focus on high-quality product photography.

## 2. Color System
- **Background**: White (`#FFFFFF`) or off-white (`#FAFAFA`)
- **Primary (Accent)**: A sophisticated, muted light pink (e.g., `#F4E1E1` or `#E8D1D1`). Do not overuse; apply strategically.
- **Primary Foreground**: Dark slate or soft black (`#1A1A1A` or `#2C2C2C`) for high contrast on primary buttons.
- **Secondary**: Light gray (`#F3F4F6`) for secondary actions or inactive states.
- **Text**: 
  - Main: `#111827` (almost black)
  - Muted: `#6B7280` (gray)
- **Status Colors**: 
  - Success: `#10B981` (emerald)
  - Error: `#EF4444` (red)
  - Discount/Sale: `#F97316` (orange) or a distinct brand color.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings**: Editorial serif font (`Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond`). Provides a premium, fashion editorial feel.
- **Body/UI**: Clean sans-serif (`Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`). Ensures readability and modern UI interactions.

## 4. Spacing & Borders
- **Spacing**: 4pt/8pt grid system. Generous padding around sections to emphasize white space.
- **Border Radius**: Subtle rounded corners (`sm` or `md` in Tailwind) or sharp edges (`none`), avoiding overly bubbly/rounded SaaS looks.

## 5. Components
- **Buttons**: Sharp or slightly rounded, solid primary background, clear hover states.
- **Cards**: Minimal borders, soft shadows only on hover or layered UI. Product cards should focus on the image.
