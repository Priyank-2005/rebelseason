# Page Specifications

## 1. Homepage (`/`)
- **Header**: Logo, Navigation, Search icon, Account icon, Cart icon.
- **Hero**: Large editorial image/video, strong typography headline, "Shop Now" CTA.
- **Featured Collections**: Grid or slider of top categories.
- **New Arrivals**: Grid of 4-8 recent products.
- **Brand Story**: Small section establishing the Rebel Season identity.
- **Footer**: Links, social icons, newsletter signup.

## 2. Shop/Category Pages (`/products`, `/categories/[slug]`)
- **Header**: Category title and description.
- **Filters**: Desktop sidebar or mobile drawer (Price, Size, Color).
- **Sorting**: Dropdown (Price low-high, Newest, etc.).
- **Product Grid**: Responsive grid (2 cols mobile, 3-4 cols desktop).

## 3. Product Detail Page (`/products/[slug]`)
- **Gallery**: Main image with thumbnails (left or bottom), zoom functionality.
- **Info Area**: 
  - Title, Price, SKU.
  - Variant Selector (Size, Color with swatches).
  - Add to Cart button (Primary).
  - Description, Material, Care instructions (Accordions).
- **Related Products**: Carousel at the bottom.

## 4. Cart & Checkout (`/cart`, `/checkout`)
- **Cart Drawer/Page**: List of items, quantity adjust, remove, subtotal, checkout CTA.
- **Checkout**: Multi-step or one-page layout.
  - Step 1: Contact & Shipping Address.
  - Step 2: Shipping Method.
  - Step 3: Payment (Razorpay trigger).

## 5. Admin Dashboard (`/admin`)
- Sidebar navigation.
- Data tables with pagination and search.
- Forms with comprehensive validation for products and categories.
